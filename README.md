## 🔭 Core Expertise & Research Areas

### 🔌 Digital Power & DSP Algorithms
- **Topologies & Controls:** Interleaved PFC (IPFC), Full-Bridge LLC, Bidirectional DC-DC / AC-DC converters.
- **Control Theory:** Peak/Average current mode control, Digital 2P2Z/3P3Z compensators, PR (Proportional Resonant) controllers, Cycle-by-cycle protection.
- **DSP Platforms:** Microchip dsPIC33 series (dsPIC33CK/CH), TI C2000 (TMS320F28x), low-latency assembly optimization & mixed-signal ADC/PWM peripherals.

### 🚗 Automotive & Embedded Systems
- **Automotive Electronics:** Body Control Modules (BCM), smart power distribution, actuator & lighting control.
- **Sensors & Mixed Signal:** AMS / AMR (Anisotropic Magneto-Resistance) magnetic sensing, current/position sensing, analog front-end (AFE) processing.
- **Protocols & Standards:** CAN / CAN-FD, LIN, functional safety principles.

### 📊 Market Analysis & Trading Research (SMC / ICT)
